A family table · Since 1981

Four decades, countless shared moments.

In January 1981, Ilde and Antonio opened the doors of Restaurant Amèrica. Their daughter Olga joined the family restaurant in 2001, and generations of guests have since made it part of their time in Fenals.

Today, the welcome is still personal and the inspiration is still Mediterranean: familiar flavours, a generous table and the pleasure of gathering with the people you love.
